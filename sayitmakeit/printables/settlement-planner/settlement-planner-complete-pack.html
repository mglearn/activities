Settlement Planner

Classroom-ready printable pack · Grades 3–8

Pack contents
fictional landscape mats, resource/building pieces, constraint cards, and tradeoff guide Two student sets are included.

Teacher directions

  1. Give partners identical cards and mats; keep the model/challenge hidden from the Builder.
  2. The Describer selects a historically scoped challenge and arranges the evidence or system.
  3. Communicate dates, locations, roles, relationships, uncertainty, and evidence precisely.
  4. Compare with the reference, correct unsupported links, and identify what the model leaves out.

Before class

  • Print at 100% on US Letter paper. Use cardstock or laminate for reuse.
  • Cut on the thick solid borders. Keep Set A and Set B separate.
  • Give challenge/model cards only to the Describer.
  • Use a folder, book, or tabletop barrier so partners cannot see each other’s work.

Accessibility and accommodations

Reduce the card set; read labels aloud or use text-to-speech; provide the vocabulary strip and a completed example. Cards pair words with symbols/patterns and include write-in options.

Settlement Planner reference

Scope, evidence, and source review

Settlement scenarios are fictional planning exercises. Real settlement histories include prior inhabitants, sovereignty, displacement, law, culture, environment, and unequal access to power and resources.

These packs simplify complex systems for barrier communication. Confirm terminology and scope against district-adopted sources before formal assessment; distinguish evidence, inference, uncertainty, and perspective.

Content review recorded 2026-08-05; educator and local-curriculum review recommended.

Quality check after a round

  • Can each student identify pieces by shape or label without relying on color?
  • Do both models match in pieces, order, orientation, and relative position?
  • Which direction was most precise? Which should be revised?
