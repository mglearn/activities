Community Map Builder

Classroom-ready printable pack · Grades 3–8

Pack contents
Street maps, building and feature cards, compass keys, and challenges. Two student sets are included.

Teacher directions

  1. Orient both maps north-up.
  2. The Describer places community features.
  3. Describe blocks, intersections, adjacency, and direction.
  4. Compare and discuss access, safety, and land use.

Before class

  • Print at 100% on US Letter paper. Use cardstock or laminate for reuse.
  • Cut on the thick solid borders. Keep Set A and Set B separate.
  • Give challenge/model cards only to the Describer.
  • Use a folder, book, or tabletop barrier so partners cannot see each other’s work.

Accessibility and accommodations

Begin with six landmarks. Preteach intersection, block, across from, and adjacent.

Community Map Builder reference

Map reference

North is at the top. Avenues are numbered west to east and streets lettered south to north. Say whether a feature occupies a block or intersection. Samples are not universal community plans.

Quality check after a round

  • Can each student identify pieces by shape or label without relying on color?
  • Do both models match in pieces, order, orientation, and relative position?
  • Which direction was most precise? Which should be revised?
