Monster Match

Classroom-ready printable pack · Grades K–8

Pack contents
Two identical sets of monster pieces; Describer-only challenge cards. Two student sets are included.

Teacher directions

  1. Give each partner one complete piece set and place a barrier between them.
  2. The Describer chooses one challenge card and builds the monster without showing it.
  3. The Describer explains number, size, shape, pattern, and position. The Builder recreates it.
  4. Remove the barrier, compare models, and revise one unclear direction. Switch roles.

Before class

  • Print at 100% on US Letter paper. Use cardstock or laminate for reuse.
  • Cut on the thick solid borders. Keep Set A and Set B separate.
  • Give challenge/model cards only to the Describer.
  • Use a folder, book, or tabletop barrier so partners cannot see each other’s work.

Accessibility and accommodations

Preteach body-part and position words; permit pointing to a vocabulary strip; reduce the piece set; provide extra response time. Pieces differ by shape, pattern, and label—not color alone.

Monster Match reference

Teacher reference

There is no single answer: a successful pair produces matching arrangements. Check body-part count, relative size, pattern, orientation, and position.

Quality check after a round

  • Can each student identify pieces by shape or label without relying on color?
  • Do both models match in pieces, order, orientation, and relative position?
  • Which direction was most precise? Which should be revised?
