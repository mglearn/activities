Activity 17 · Arts & Design

Back-to-Back Drawing

GradesK–8
STEAM🎨 Arts & Design
Materials Simple line drawings, paper, and pencils.

What to describe

Partners sit back-to-back. One describes a picture while the other draws it without seeing the original.

Roles

How a round works

Make it harder or easier

Harder

Describe using only shape, position, and color words — no gestures, no questions.

Easier

Allow yes/no questions and agree on a shared starting shape or anchor point.
