Activity 59 · Arts & Design

Vocabulary Object Match

GradesK–8
STEAM🎨 Arts & Design
Materials Two identical sets of curriculum-related objects or cards.

What to describe

The Describer names a hidden STEAM object — a geometric solid, a lab tool, a labeled specimen — using precise vocabulary until the Builder selects the matching one from a shared set.

Roles

How a round works

Make it harder or easier

Harder

Describe using only shape, position, and color words — no gestures, no questions.

Easier

Allow yes/no questions and agree on a shared starting shape or anchor point.
