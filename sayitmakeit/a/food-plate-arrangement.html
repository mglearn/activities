Food-Plate Arrangement

GradesK–8
STEAM🎨 Arts & Design
Materials Paper plates and matching food cards.

What to describe

One student creates a meal or nutrition plate and directs a partner in duplicating it.

Roles

How a round works

Make it harder or easier

Harder

Describe using only shape, position, and color words — no gestures, no questions.

Easier

Allow yes/no questions and agree on a shared starting shape or anchor point.
