Activity 3 · Engineering

One-Message Cup Challenge

GradesK–5
STEAM🏗️ Engineering
Materials Matching cup sets and a barrier.
Materials decision Runs with common classroom manipulatives; no custom printable is required.

What to describe

The Describer studies the model and may give only one uninterrupted set of directions. The Builder cannot ask questions until the description is complete.

Roles

How a round works

Make it harder or easier

Harder

Go one-way — ban questions — and add a 30-word limit; require measured lengths and units.

Easier

Allow yes/no questions, agree on a shared base piece, and let the Builder peek once before the barrier goes up.
