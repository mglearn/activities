Rock-Layer Reconstruction

Grades3–8
STEAM🔬 Science
Materials Colored strips or sediment-layer cards.

What to describe

The Describer arranges geological layers, fossils, and faults. The partner reproduces the cross-section.

Roles

How a round works

Harder

Easier
