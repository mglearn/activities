Activity 37 ·

Story Scene Match

GradesK–8
STEAM🎨 Arts & Design
Materials Story setting mats and character or object pieces.

What to describe

Students create and copy a scene from a familiar story. Afterward, they narrate what might be happening.

Roles

How a round works

Make it harder or easier

Harder

Go one-way — no questions allowed — or cap the Describer at thirty words. Add a Messenger who cannot see either model.

Easier

Allow yes/no clarifying questions, agree on a shared starting piece, and let the Builder peek once before the barrier goes up.
