Playground Designer

GradesK–8
STEAM🎨 Arts & Design
Materials Playground mats and matching picture pieces.

What to describe

One student creates a playground scene and directs a partner to place slides, trees, benches, paths, and play areas.

Roles

How a round works

Make it harder or easier

Harder

Describe using only shape, position, and color words — no gestures, no questions.

Easier

Allow yes/no questions and agree on a shared starting shape or anchor point.
