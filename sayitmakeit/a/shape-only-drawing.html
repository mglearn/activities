Shape-Only Drawing

GradesK–8
STEAM🎨 Arts & Design
Materials Geometric drawings and paper.

What to describe

The Describer may use only shape, size, line, angle, and position words. The Describer cannot name familiar objects.

Roles

How a round works

Make it harder or easier

Harder

Describe using only shape, position, and color words — no gestures, no questions.

Easier

Allow yes/no questions and agree on a shared starting shape or anchor point.
