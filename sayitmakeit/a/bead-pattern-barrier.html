Grades2–8
STEAM📐 Mathematics
Materials Matching beads, strings, and a barrier.

What to describe

One student creates a color, shape, or size sequence and describes it for a partner to reproduce.

Roles

How a round works

Make it harder or easier

Harder

Require coordinates, units, and exact quantities; describe in one uninterrupted turn with no questions.

Easier

Allow yes/no questions and provide a labeled grid or number line as a shared reference.
