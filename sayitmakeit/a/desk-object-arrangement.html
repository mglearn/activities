Activity 29 · Arts & Design

Desk-Object Arrangement

GradesK–8
STEAM🎨 Arts & Design
Materials Identical sets of five to eight classroom objects.
Materials decision Runs with common classroom manipulatives; no custom printable is required.

What to describe

One student arranges the objects and explains how a partner should create the same arrangement.

Roles

How a round works

Make it harder or easier

Harder

Describe using only shape, position, and color words — no gestures, no questions.

Easier

Allow yes/no questions and agree on a shared starting shape or anchor point.
