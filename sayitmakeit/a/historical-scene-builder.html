Historical Scene Builder

GradesK–8
STEAM🎨 Arts & Design
Materials Background mats and historically appropriate people, buildings, and object cards.

What to describe

The Describer arranges a historical scene and directs a partner to duplicate it.

Roles

How a round works

Make it harder or easier

Harder

Describe using only shape, position, and color words — no gestures, no questions.

Easier

Allow yes/no questions and agree on a shared starting shape or anchor point.

Reflect

Skills
