Which Picture Am I Describing?

GradesK–8
STEAM🎨 Arts & Design
Materials Closely related image sets.

What to describe

From a set of similar scientific diagrams or graphs, the Describer specifies one precisely enough that the Builder can pick exactly the one being described.

Roles

How a round works

Make it harder or easier

Harder

Describe using only shape, position, and color words — no gestures, no questions.

Easier

Allow yes/no questions and agree on a shared starting shape or anchor point.
