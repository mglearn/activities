Create a Habitat

Grades3–8
STEAM🔬 Science
Materials Habitat backgrounds and animal, plant, water, shelter, and food pieces.

What to describe

One student constructs a habitat scene. The partner recreates it while discussing where organisms belong.

Roles

How a round works

Make it harder or easier

Harder

Use only precise science vocabulary; one-way delivery, no questions allowed.

Easier

Allow a labeled diagram and yes/no clarifying questions.
