Activity 48 · Mathematics

Mystery Sorting Rule

Grades2–8
STEAM📐 Mathematics
Materials Matching sets of word, image, shape, or number cards.

What to describe

The Describer explains where each item belongs but cannot state the category labels. The partner infers the rule.

Roles

How a round works

Make it harder or easier

Harder

Require coordinates, units, and exact quantities; describe in one uninterrupted turn with no questions.

Easier

Allow yes/no questions and provide a labeled grid or number line as a shared reference.

Reflect

Skills
