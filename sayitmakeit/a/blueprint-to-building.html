Blueprint to Building

GradesK–5
STEAM🏗️ Engineering
Materials Blocks and a simple blueprint or diagram.

What to describe

One student sees a top, front, or side-view drawing and describes it so the Builder can produce the three-dimensional model.

Roles

How a round works

Make it harder or easier

Harder

Go one-way — ban questions — and add a 30-word limit; require measured lengths and units.

Easier

Allow yes/no questions, agree on a shared base piece, and let the Builder peek once before the barrier goes up.
