Activity 57 ·

Grid Robot Programmer

Grades2–8
STEAM🤖 Technology
Materials Grid mats, tokens, and obstacle cards.

What to describe

The Programmer gives step-by-step commands to move a token through the grid. The Operator follows the commands exactly.

Roles

How a round works

Make it harder or easier

Harder

Give the entire algorithm up front as numbered steps; no live questions or corrections.

Easier

Allow the Builder to confirm and act on one instruction at a time.
