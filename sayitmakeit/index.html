Say It, Make It

One partner describes a hidden model, drawing, pattern, or setup — using words only. The other builds it. Then compare, and talk about what made the words work. 106 STEAM activities.

🔁 Facilitation Modes 16

91★ Facilitation Modes

No-Questions Round

The Listener may not interrupt or ask for clarification.
92★ Facilitation Modes

Questions-Allowed Round

The Listener may ask as many clarifying questions as needed.
93★ Facilitation Modes

Yes-or-No Questions Only

The Listener may ask questions, but the Describer can answer only yes or no.
94★ Facilitation Modes

Thirty-Word Limit

The Describer may use no more than thirty words.
95★ Facilitation Modes

One Direction at a Time

The Builder must complete and confirm each step before receiving the next direction.
96★ Facilitation Modes

Memory Messenger

The Messenger cannot take notes and may carry only one message per trip.
97★ Facilitation Modes

Written Messenger

The Architect writes each message. The Messenger delivers it without explanation.
98★ Facilitation Modes

Multiple Messengers

Information passes through two or three students before reaching the Builder.
99★ Facilitation Modes

Competing Voices

Two students provide directions, but only one has accurate information.
100★ Facilitation Modes

Communication-Code Challenge

Teams receive planning time to create abbreviations, coordinates, labels, or shared terms before seeing the structure.
101★ Facilitation Modes

Accuracy Before Speed

Teams receive points for exact matches and lose points for finishing with errors.
102★ Facilitation Modes

Speed Before Accuracy

Teams try to finish quickly, then calculate an accuracy score.
103★ Facilitation Modes

Observer Feedback Round

An Observer records examples of clear language, vague directions, questions, and corrections.
104★ Facilitation Modes

Teach-Back Rule

After each direction, the Builder must restate it before acting.
105★ Facilitation Modes

Error-Recovery Challenge

The teacher secretly moves or replaces one piece during the activity. The team must detect and repair the discrepancy.
106★ Facilitation Modes

Student-Designed Challenge

Students create models, drawings, maps, or patterns for other groups to reproduce. ---
