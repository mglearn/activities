Track A · Grades 6–8

Lab Safety Ranger

Five short modules build the habits every Texas science student needs: rules, gear, emergency equipment, hazard signs, and spotting danger. Finish all five to earn the Ranger badge.

Track B · Grades 9–12

Lab Safety Specialist

Higher-rigor modules for Biology, Chemistry, Physics, and IPC: read Safety Data Sheets, classify hazards with GHS pictograms, judge chemical compatibility, and critique a flawed procedure before it becomes an accident.

Certification

Your Certificate

Progress is saved on this device only — nothing is uploaded or shared. Complete every module in a track to unlock its certificate. Print it or save it as a PDF for your teacher.
