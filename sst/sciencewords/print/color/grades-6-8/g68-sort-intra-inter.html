Sort It: Intra or Inter

Grades 6–8 · Organisms and Environments · MOY · Connect · Sort It · 10 min
NameDate
INTRA and INTER look almost the same but mean different things. Sort each description into the column that fits.

Word parts you will need

Prefix INTRA within, inside one thing
Prefix INTER between things
Word CELLULAR relating to a cell

Within one cell, or between cells?

INTRA- (within one cell)

INTER- (between cells)

Sort these

  • fluid inside a single cell
  • a signal passed from one cell to the next
  • reactions happening inside one cell
  • the gap between two cells
  • digestion within a single cell
  • water moving between leaf cells
Explain the difference between INTRA and INTER in your own words.
Interstate highways run between states. Does that fit INTER? Explain.
