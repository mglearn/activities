A word equation shows the pieces adding up to a whole word. Fill in the missing pieces. Then write the science meaning — it is usually more than just the two parts added together.

Word-part clue: Science meaning:
The word-part clue and the science meaning are not the same thing. A thermometer does not measure heat itself — it measures temperature. The clue got you close. The science makes it exact.
Look at the note above. Why is "a heat measurer" a good clue but not a perfect definition?
GEOTHERMAL uses THERM too. GEO means Earth. What do you predict GEOTHERMAL means?
