Decode the Stimulus: Geothermal (EOY)

Grades 3–5 · Earth and Space · EOY · Extend · Decode the Stimulus · 20 min
NameDate
You have not studied this word in class. Work through all five steps. Use the word parts and the evidence together — neither one is enough by itself.
GEOTHERMAL
GEO  +  THERM  +  -AL
Step 1: Break it. What word parts do you recognize, and what does each one mean?
Step 2: Predict. What might GEOTHERMAL mean?

Step 3: Check the science

A cross-section of the ground beneath a small town. Water moves down through cracks in the rock, is heated by hot rock below, and rises back to the surface as hot water and steam.

Ground temperature at three depths

Depth below surfaceTemperature
0 meters (surface)18 °C
500 meters40 °C
1,500 meters95 °C
Step 4: Explain. What evidence in the diagram and the table supports or changes your prediction? Write your final meaning of GEOTHERMAL.

A town wants to heat its buildings without burning fuel. Based on the diagram and the table, which plan makes the most sense?

  1. Pump water down to hot rock, then bring the heated water back up to the buildings.
  2. Store cold water at the surface until the Sun warms it.
  3. Leave barrels of water on the surface overnight and use them in the morning.
  4. Move the town to a place with warmer weather.
What evidence helped you decide?
Step 5: Extend. HYDROELECTRIC power uses moving water instead of heat. Both are called RENEWABLE. What do you think RENEWABLE means, and why do both belong in that group?
