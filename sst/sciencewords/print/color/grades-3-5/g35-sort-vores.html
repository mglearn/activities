Sort It: Herbivore, Carnivore, Insectivore

Grades 3–5 · Organisms and Environments · MOY · Connect · Sort It · 15 min
NameDate
All three words end in VORE. Use the first part of each word to decide where each animal belongs. Write the animal in the correct column.

Word parts

Root HERB plant
Root CARN meat
Word INSECT insect
Suffix VORE eater

Sort the animals

HERBIVORE plant eater

CARNIVORE meat eater

INSECTIVORE insect eater

Sort these

  • grasshopper eating grass
  • snake eating a frog
  • bat catching moths
  • cow eating hay
  • hawk eating a mouse
  • anteater eating ants
  • rabbit eating clover
  • frog catching flies

Use the food chain

Sun→grass→grasshopper→frog→snake
Which animal in the chain is a herbivore? How do you know from the word part?
The frog eats insects. Is the frog a carnivore, an insectivore, or both? Explain your thinking.
If the grasshoppers disappeared, what would happen to the frogs? Use the chain as evidence.
