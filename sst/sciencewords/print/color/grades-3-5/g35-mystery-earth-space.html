Mystery Words: Earth and Space

Grades 3–5 · Earth and Space · MOY · Connect · Mystery Word · 15 min
NameDate
Each card shows a mystery word and the clues inside it. Predict the meaning, circle the clue that helped you most, then read the science line and decide whether to keep or change your prediction.

Mystery words

TELESCOPE
TELE = far off
SCOPE = look at
My prediction:
Science: It gathers light from objects that are very far away.
ASTRONOMY
ASTRO = star, space
-NOMY = arrangement
My prediction:
Science: It studies the objects in space and the patterns in how they move.
GEOSPHERE
GEO = Earth, rock
SPHERE = region, layer
My prediction:
Science: It is Earth's solid rock and soil, at the surface and far below it.
SUBSOIL
SUB = below
SOIL = soil
My prediction:
Science: It is the layer found beneath the darker topsoil, with more clay and small rocks.
Which mystery word was easiest to predict? Why did the clues work so well for that one?
Which one needed the science line to get right? What did the science line add?
