Decode It: Hydrosphere

HYDROSPHERE
HYDRO  +  SPHERE

Step 1: Break it

Root HYDRO water
Suffix SPHERE region, layer
Step 2: Predict. What do you think HYDROSPHERE means?

Step 3: Check the science

Earth's water is not only in the ocean. Water sits in lakes and rivers, moves underground through soil and rock, freezes into glaciers and ice caps, and floats in the air as water vapor. Scientists group all of this water together and call it the hydrosphere.

Step 4: Explain. Was your prediction close? Write the meaning of HYDROSPHERE using the passage.
Which of these are part of the hydrosphere? Check all that the passage supports.
  • The ocean
  • Water vapor in the air
  • A glacier
  • Rock deep underground
  • Water moving through soil
