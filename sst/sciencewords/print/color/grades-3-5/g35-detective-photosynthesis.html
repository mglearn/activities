Observation notes: the closet plant

Two bean plants started the same. Plant A stayed on a sunny windowsill. Plant B went into a dark closet. Both got the same water. After two weeks, Plant A was taller and dark green. Plant B was pale, thin, and leaning toward the door. Under a hand lens the leaves of Plant A were thick; the leaves of Plant B were thin and papery. The class had learned that plants make their own food through PHOTOSYNTHESIS, and that leaves have MICROSCOPIC openings that let gases move in and out.
