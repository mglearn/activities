Word-part clue: Science meaning:
Electrolysis uses an electric current to split a compound. Passing electricity through water breaks it into hydrogen gas and oxygen gas.
Which part of ELECTROLYSIS tells you what does the breaking? Which part tells you a breaking happens?
HYDROLYSIS breaks with water and PHOTOLYSIS breaks with light. Using the first parts, predict what breaks each substance.
