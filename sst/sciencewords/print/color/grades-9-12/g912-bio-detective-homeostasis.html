Holding steady

On a hot afternoon a runner's body works to stay near 37 °C. As she heats up, she sweats, and the evaporating water cools her skin. Meanwhile her blood GLUCOSE rises after a snack, and her body releases a signal that moves the extra sugar into cells, bringing the level back down. Whatever the outside conditions, the internal state is pulled back toward a steady point. Scientists call this balancing act HOMEOSTASIS.
