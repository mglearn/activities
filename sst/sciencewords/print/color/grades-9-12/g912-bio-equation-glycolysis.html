Word-part clue: Science meaning:
Glycolysis is the first step of cellular respiration. It splits one glucose molecule into smaller molecules and releases a little usable energy. The sugar it splits, GLUCOSE, ends in -OSE, which marks a sugar.
Which part of GLYCOLYSIS tells you a sugar is involved? Which part tells you it is broken?
GLUCOSE, LACTOSE, and RIBOSE all end in -OSE. What do all three have in common?
