Build It: Word-Part Cards

Grades 3–5 · Organisms and Environments · BOY · Articulate · Build It · 15 min
NameDate
Cut out the ten cards below. Put a first-part card next to a last-part card to build a real science word. Write down every real word you can build, then write what it means.

Cut apart these cards
