Read the science

Earth's systems are separated for study, but they touch each other constantly. The atmosphere is the layer of gases held around Earth by gravity. Warm air rising off the ocean carries water vapor upward. The vapor cools, condenses into clouds, and later falls as rain onto soil and rock, where plant roots take it in.

The passage describes rain falling on soil. Which two systems is that? Explain how you decided.
A student says the atmosphere and the hydrosphere never touch. Use the passage to explain why that is not correct.
