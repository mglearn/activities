Sort It: Genotype or Phenotype

Grades 9–12 · Biology · MOY · Connect · Sort It · 15 min
NameDate
GENO points to the genes an organism carries; PHENO points to what shows. Sort each description into the column it fits.

Word parts you will need

Root GENO gene, hereditary makeup
Root PHENO showing, appearance
Word TYPE kind, form

Which does each describe?

Genotype — the genes carried

Phenotype — the traits shown

Sort these

  • the alleles Bb
  • brown eyes
  • the DNA sequence for a trait
  • a six-foot-tall plant
  • TT for seed shape
  • a wrinkled pea pod
Both words share the root TYPE. What does that tell you both words are describing?
Two plants show the same purple flowers but have genotypes BB and Bb. Explain how their phenotype can match while their genotype differs.
