Sort It: -ide, -ate, or -ite

Grades 9–12 · Chemistry · MOY · Connect · Sort It · 15 min
NameDate
The ending of a chemical name is a clue. Sort each name into the column its ending points to.

What each ending signals

Suffix -IDE just two elements
Suffix -ATE oxygen — the richer form
Suffix -ITE oxygen — the poorer form

Sort by the ending

-IDE — two elements

-ATE — oxygen (more)

-ITE — oxygen (less)

Sort these

  • chloride
  • sulfate
  • sulfite
  • nitrate
  • nitrite
  • sulfide
  • carbonate
  • oxide
Sulfate is SO₄²⁻ and sulfite is SO₃²⁻. How does the ending predict which one has more oxygen?
A name ends in -IDE. What can you say about how many kinds of element it contains?
