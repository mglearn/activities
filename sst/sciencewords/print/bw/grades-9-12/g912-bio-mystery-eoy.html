Mystery Science Word (EOY): -ase and -ose

Grades 9–12 · Biology · EOY · Extend · Mystery Word · 20 min
NameDate
You have not studied these exact words. Use two reliable endings — -ASE for enzymes and -OSE for sugars — to work them out. Then check against the passage.

Word parts you will need

Suffix -ASE an enzyme
Suffix -OSE a sugar

Reading the endings

In the small intestine, the sugar SUCROSE cannot be absorbed until it is broken into smaller sugars. An enzyme called SUCRASE does that job. The same pattern repeats elsewhere: the enzyme MALTASE breaks the sugar MALTOSE, and the enzyme AMYLASE breaks starch into sugars. The endings tell you which is which before you know anything else about them.
