Mystery Science Word (EOY): Chemosynthesis

Grades 6–8 · Organisms and Environments · EOY · Extend · Mystery Word · 20 min

You have not studied this word in class. Work through every step. Use the word parts and the passage together — neither is enough alone.
CHEMOSYNTHESIS
CHEMO  +  SYN  +  THESIS

Word parts you will need

Root CHEMO chemical
Prefix SYN together
Root THESIS putting, placing
Step 1: Break it. What word parts do you recognize, and what does each mean?
Step 2: Predict. What might CHEMOSYNTHESIS mean?

Step 3: Check the science

At the bottom of the ocean, no sunlight reaches. Yet around cracks in the sea floor that release hot, chemical-rich water, whole communities of tube worms and bacteria thrive. The bacteria do not use light. They build their food using energy released from chemicals in the water, and the tube worms depend on them.

Step 4: Explain. Using the word parts and the passage, write your final meaning of CHEMOSYNTHESIS.

How is chemosynthesis different from photosynthesis?

  1. It uses energy from chemicals instead of energy from light.
  2. It uses light instead of chemicals.
  3. It does not make food at all.
  4. It only happens in plants.
