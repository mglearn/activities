Word-part clue: Science meaning:
Hydrolysis uses a water molecule to split a larger molecule into smaller pieces. Your body uses it to break food apart during digestion.
Which part of HYDROLYSIS tells you what does the breaking?
During digestion, a starch molecule is broken into sugars with the help of water. Explain how the word HYDROLYSIS matches that.
