Lab notes: the onion skin

A student peeled a thin, clear layer from an onion and placed it on a slide. Under the microscope, each block-shaped cell was filled with clear CYTOPLASM. A drop of coloured dye slowly moved into some cells but not others, because the cell membrane is SEMIPERMEABLE. The onion skin had no green colour, so the student knew these cells held little or no chlorophyll.
