LITHOSPHERE
LITH  +  O  +  SPHERE

Word parts you will need

Root LITH rock, stone
Root SPHERE ball or layer
Break it: what does each part mean?
Predict: what do you think the lithosphere is?
The lithosphere is the solid, rocky outer layer of the Earth — the crust and the top of the mantle. It is cracked into plates that move slowly.
HYDROSPHERE and ATMOSPHERE also end in SPHERE. Using their first parts, what material do you expect each to be made of?
