Sort It: Autotroph or Heterotroph

Grades 6–8 · Organisms and Environments · MOY · Connect · Sort It · 15 min
NameDate
Both key words end in TROPH, which is about feeding. Sort each living thing into the column that fits how it gets its food.

Word parts you will need

Prefix AUTO self
Prefix HETERO other, different
Root TROPH nourishment, feeding

Sort by how it gets food

Autotroph — makes its own food

Heterotroph — eats other living things

Sort these

  • grass
  • rabbit
  • oak tree
  • mushroom
  • algae
  • hawk
  • cactus
  • human
TROPH appears in both words. What does that tell you both words are about?
A Venus flytrap makes food by photosynthesis but also traps insects. Why is it hard to place in just one column?
