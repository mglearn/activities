Partner Challenge: Build and Trade

Grades 6–8 · Organisms and Environments · EOY · Extend · Partner Challenge · 15 min
NameDate
Work with a partner. Cut out the parts. Take turns: one partner builds a science word from two or more parts; the other breaks it back apart and predicts the meaning. Check each other with the word bank.
