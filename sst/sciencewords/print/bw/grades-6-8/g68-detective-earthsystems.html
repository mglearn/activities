One storm, four systems

Clouds build over a valley and rain begins to fall. The water starts in the ATMOSPHERE. It lands on bare rock and soil, part of the LITHOSPHERE, and runs downhill into a stream that joins the HYDROSPHERE. Along the way, plant roots and thirsty animals in the BIOSPHERE take up some of the water. A single storm moves water through all four systems.
