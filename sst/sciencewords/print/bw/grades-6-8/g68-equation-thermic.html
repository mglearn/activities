Word-part clue: Which way does heat move?:
The direction part tells you which way heat moves, not the temperature you feel. In an endothermic change the surroundings give up heat to the change, so the surroundings feel colder.
An instant cold pack feels cold when you squeeze it. Which word describes it, and which way does heat move?
A hand warmer feels hot. Which word, and which way does heat move?
