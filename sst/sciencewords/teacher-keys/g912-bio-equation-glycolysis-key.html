Word Equation: Glycolysis

Grades 9–12 · Biology · MOY · Connect · Word Equation · 15 min

Teacher key

  • GLYCOLYSIS equation
    GLYCO = sugar, LYSIS = breaking apart; splitting a sugar (glucose) to release energy.
  • Which part means sugar / broken
    GLYCO signals the sugar; LYSIS signals that it is broken apart.
    Accept either order if both parts are mapped.
  • What -OSE words share
    All are sugars. The -OSE ending marks a sugar, so glucose, lactose, and ribose are all sugars.
    Reward the suffix generalization.
  • PHOTOLYSIS
    Splitting (LYSIS) using light (PHOTO). Water is broken apart by light.
    Transfer of LYSIS with a new first part.

What to look for

A level 3 answer maps both parts onto glucose and connects the -OSE ending to 'sugar'. The photolysis item checks transfer of LYSIS.

Response evidence

1 — One clueIdentifies one relevant word part.
2 — Several cluesIdentifies multiple parts but does not connect them.
3 — Connected meaningCombines word structure and science context.
4 — TransferUses known word parts successfully in a new situation.
