Mystery Words: Earth and Space

Grades 3–5 · Earth and Space · MOY · Connect · Mystery Word · 15 min

Teacher key

  • TELESCOPE
    A tool for looking at things far away.
    Both clues point the same direction, which is why students usually name this one as easiest.
  • ASTRONOMY
    The study of objects in space and how they move.
    -NOMY is not transparent to elementary students. This card usually needs the science line, which is the intended lesson.
  • GEOSPHERE
    Earth's rock and soil, at the surface and far below.
  • SUBSOIL
    The soil layer beneath the topsoil.
  • Which needed the science line?
    Usually ASTRONOMY. The clue ASTRO is strong but -NOMY does not tell a student it means a field of study.
  • ACE Extend: subsurface
    Below the surface — underground.

What to look for

Ask students to circle the strongest clue before writing. Students who circle a clue and then ignore it in their prediction need a prompt to connect the two.

Response evidence

1 — One clueIdentifies one relevant word part.
2 — Several cluesIdentifies multiple parts but does not connect them.
3 — Connected meaningCombines word structure and science context.
4 — TransferUses known word parts successfully in a new situation.
