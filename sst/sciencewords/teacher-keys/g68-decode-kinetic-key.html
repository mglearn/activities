Decode It: Kinetic Energy

Grades 6–8 · Force, Motion, and Energy · MOY · Connect · Decode It · 15 min

Teacher key

  • Break it
    KINET = motion; -IC = relating to. Having to do with motion.
  • Predict
    Energy that has to do with movement.
    Accept any prediction that links the word to motion or moving.
  • Kinetic energy as the cart speeds up
    It rises as the cart moves faster: 0 J at rest, then 2, 8, and 18 J. Final meaning: the energy an object has because it is moving, and it is greater at higher speed.
    Require the trend from the table, not just 'it moves'.
  • Parked car
    The parked car has no motion, so its kinetic energy is zero. Kinetic energy depends on movement.
    Correct choice is the first option.
  • Bowling ball vs tennis ball
    The bowling ball, because it has more mass. Kinetic energy depends on both speed and mass, so at the same speed the heavier object has more.
    Transfer item: the single-cart table held mass constant, so this checks whether the student adds the mass factor.

What to look for

A level 3 answer ties KINET to the table's rising energy. The bowling-ball item checks transfer to mass — the factor the table deliberately held constant.
