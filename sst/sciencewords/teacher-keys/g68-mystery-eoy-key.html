Mystery Science Word (EOY): Chemosynthesis

Grades 6–8 · Organisms and Environments · EOY · Extend · Mystery Word · 20 min

Teacher key

  • Step 1: Break it
    CHEMO = chemical; SYN = together; THESIS = putting or placing. Putting together using chemicals.
  • Step 2: Predict
    Making something by putting parts together using chemicals.
    Accept any reasonable prediction from the parts.
  • Step 4: Final meaning
    Building food using energy from chemicals instead of light. Evidence: the deep-sea bacteria make food with no sunlight, using energy from chemicals in the vent water.
    Require the passage cited, not parts alone.
  • Different from photosynthesis
    Chemosynthesis uses energy from chemicals; photosynthesis uses energy from light. PHOTO = light, CHEMO = chemical.
    Correct answer is the first option; reward the PHOTO/CHEMO contrast.
  • Cave microbe
    It most likely uses chemosynthesis — building food from the sulfur chemicals, since there is no light for photosynthesis.
    This is the transfer item. A level 4 answer uses both the parts and the no-light evidence.

What to look for

The end-of-year target is transfer: applying CHEMO vs PHOTO to a new no-light setting. A student who answers from the parts alone cannot rule out photosynthesis in the cave.

Response evidence

2 — Several cluesIdentifies multiple parts but does not connect them.
3 — Connected meaningCombines word structure and science context.
