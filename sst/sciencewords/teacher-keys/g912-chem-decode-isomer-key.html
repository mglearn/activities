Decode It: Isomer

Grades 9–12 · Chemistry · MOY · Connect · Decode It · 15 min

Teacher key

  • Break it
    ISO = same, MER = part; same parts.
  • Predict
    Molecules with the same pieces but possibly arranged differently.
    Accept any prediction using 'same' plus 'arrangement/parts'.
  • Same and different in the table
    Same: the formula C₆H₁₂O₆ for both. Different: the arrangement (ring shape) and the resulting taste. Final meaning: compounds with the same formula but different structures.
    Require the identical formula read from the table.
  • Why they differ
    Their atoms are arranged differently, even though the formula matches.
    Correct choice is the first option.
  • C₂H₆O isomers
    ISOMER lets you predict there is another compound with the same formula but a different structure and different properties.
    Transfer: same formula need not mean same substance.

What to look for

The decode behaviour is tying ISO to the identical formula and MER to arrangement. A student who says isomers are 'the same molecule' has missed the structural difference.

Response evidence

1 — One clueIdentifies one relevant word part.
2 — Several cluesIdentifies multiple parts but does not connect them.
3 — Connected meaningCombines word structure and science context.
4 — TransferUses known word parts successfully in a new situation.
