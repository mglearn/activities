Word Equation: Electrolysis

Grades 9–12 · Chemistry · MOY · Connect · Word Equation · 15 min

Teacher key

  • ELECTROLYSIS equation
    ELECTRO = electricity, LYSIS = breaking apart; using electricity to split a compound.
  • Which part breaks / signals breaking
    ELECTRO says electricity does the work; LYSIS says a breaking happens.
    Accept either order if both are mapped.
  • HYDROLYSIS and PHOTOLYSIS
    Hydrolysis breaks a substance with water (HYDRO); photolysis breaks it with light (PHOTO). The first part names what does the breaking.
    Reward the -LYSIS family generalization.
  • THERMOLYSIS
    Heat (THERMO) supplies the energy to break the compound.
    Transfer of the pattern to a new first part.

What to look for

A level 3 answer maps both parts and generalizes the -LYSIS family by first part. The thermolysis item checks transfer.

Response evidence

1 — One clueIdentifies one relevant word part.
2 — Several cluesIdentifies multiple parts but does not connect them.
3 — Connected meaningCombines word structure and science context.
4 — TransferUses known word parts successfully in a new situation.
