Word Equation: Microscope, and the MICRO Problem

Grades 3–5 · Organisms and Environments · BOY · Articulate · Word Equation · 10 min

Teacher key

  • MICROSCOPE
    Clue: a small-looker. Science meaning: a tool that uses lenses to make very small objects look larger.
  • TELESCOPE
    Clue: a far-looker. Science meaning: a tool that gathers light from distant objects so they can be seen more clearly.
  • Odd word out
    microwave.
    In microwave, MICRO refers to a short wavelength, not to a small oven or small food. The other three all involve smallness.
  • What does this teach you?
    Word parts are clues, not guarantees. You still have to check the meaning against the science.
    Accept any wording that carries the caution.
  • ACE Extend: macroscopic
    No tool needed — you can see it with your eyes.

What to look for

The odd-word-out item is the point of the page. A student who circles microwave but cannot say why is at level 1.

Response evidence

2 — Several cluesIdentifies multiple parts but does not connect them.
3 — Connected meaningCombines word structure and science context.
