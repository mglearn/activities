Exit Ticket: Morphology Check (BOY)

Grades 6–8 · Organisms and Environments · BOY · Articulate · Exit Ticket · 5 min

Teacher key

  • CYTO means
    cell.
  • So CYTOPLASM probably means
    the material inside a cell (cell + formed material).
    Accept any answer that uses 'cell'.
  • What clue did CYTO give you?
    That the word is about a cell.

What to look for

A 30-second read. A student who writes 'cell' for CYTO and uses it in the prediction is at least level 2.

Response evidence

1 — One clueIdentifies one relevant word part.
2 — Several cluesIdentifies multiple parts but does not connect them.
3 — Connected meaningCombines word structure and science context.
4 — TransferUses known word parts successfully in a new situation.
