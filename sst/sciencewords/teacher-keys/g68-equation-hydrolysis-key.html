Word Equation: Hydrolysis

Grades 6–8 · Matter and Energy · MOY · Connect · Word Equation · 15 min

Teacher key

  • HYDROLYSIS equation
    HYDRO = water, LYSIS = breaking apart; breaking a molecule apart using water.
  • Which part does the breaking?
    LYSIS names the breaking apart; HYDRO says water is what does it.
    Accept 'LYSIS' if the student explains it means breaking.
  • Starch to sugars
    Water splits the large starch molecule into separate sugar units, which is exactly what HYDRO + LYSIS describes.
    Look for the parts mapped onto the digestion example.
  • ANALYSIS
    Breaking a problem down into parts to understand it — the same 'breaking apart' idea.
    Transfer of the LYSIS root beyond science.

What to look for

A level 3 answer maps both parts onto the digestion example. Reward the LYSIS transfer to analysis.

Response evidence

1 — One clueIdentifies one relevant word part.
2 — Several cluesIdentifies multiple parts but does not connect them.
3 — Connected meaningCombines word structure and science context.
4 — TransferUses known word parts successfully in a new situation.
