Word Detective: Inside the Cell

Grades 6–8 · Organisms and Environments · MOY · Connect · Word Detective · 20 min

Teacher key

  • CYTOPLASM
    CYTO = cell, PLASM = formed material; the clear jelly filling each cell. Evidence: the notes say each cell was 'filled with clear cytoplasm'.
    Require the evidence column to cite the notes, not just a definition.
  • SEMIPERMEABLE
    SEMI = partly, PERMEABLE = able to pass through; a membrane that lets some things through but not others. Evidence: the dye entered some cells but not others.
  • Why dye entered some cells
    The membrane is semipermeable, so it lets only certain particles through; the dye crossed where it could and was blocked where it could not.
    Look for SEMI used to explain 'some but not all'.
  • Not green
    There is little or no chlorophyll in onion-skin cells, since chlorophyll is the green pigment.

What to look for

The detective behaviour is revising a prediction using the notes. A student who fills 'final meaning' without touching the evidence column is at level 2.

Response evidence

1 — One clueIdentifies one relevant word part.
2 — Several cluesIdentifies multiple parts but does not connect them.
3 — Connected meaningCombines word structure and science context.
4 — TransferUses known word parts successfully in a new situation.
