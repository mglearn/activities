Decode It: Lithosphere

Grades 6–8 · Earth and Space · BOY · Articulate · Decode It · 10 min

Teacher key

  • Break it
    LITH = rock or stone; SPHERE = ball or layer.
  • Predict
    A rocky ball or layer — the rocky layer of the Earth.
    Accept any prediction close to 'rock layer'.
  • HYDROSPHERE and ATMOSPHERE
    Hydrosphere = water layer (HYDRO = water); atmosphere = air layer (ATMO = air or vapor).
    Look for the first part driving the material named.

What to look for

A level 3 answer uses LITH to justify 'rocky' rather than guessing. The -SPHERE generalization is the transfer target.

Response evidence

1 — One clueIdentifies one relevant word part.
2 — Several cluesIdentifies multiple parts but does not connect them.
3 — Connected meaningCombines word structure and science context.
4 — TransferUses known word parts successfully in a new situation.
