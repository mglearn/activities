Break It: Chlorophyll

Grades 6–8 · Organisms and Environments · BOY · Articulate · Break It · 10 min

Teacher key

  • CHLOROPHYLL
    CHLORO = green; PHYLL = leaf; the green substance in leaves.
    Accept 'green pigment in plants'.
  • CHLOROPLAST
    CHLORO = green; PLAST = formed body; the green structure inside a plant cell that holds chlorophyll.
  • What colour, and where?
    Green, in the leaf or plant cell. CHLORO signals green.
  • How are chlorophyll and chloroplast related?
    The chloroplast is the structure; the chlorophyll is the green pigment inside it. Both share CHLORO because both involve the green light-capturing system.
    Look for the part-to-whole relationship, not just two definitions.

What to look for

A strong answer connects the two words through the shared CHLORO and explains the structure-versus-pigment difference.
