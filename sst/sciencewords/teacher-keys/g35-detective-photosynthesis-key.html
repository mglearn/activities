Word Detective: Photosynthesis

Grades 3–5 · Organisms and Environments · MOY · Connect · Word Detective · 20 min

Teacher key

  • PHOTOSYNTHESIS — parts
    PHOTO = light; SYN = together; THESIS = putting or placing.
  • PHOTOSYNTHESIS — final meaning
    The process a plant uses to make its own food using light energy, water, and carbon dioxide, releasing oxygen.
    Grade-band answer: making food using light. Do not require the full equation.
  • PHOTOSYNTHESIS — evidence from the passage
    Plant B had water but no light and grew pale and thin; Plant A had light and grew tall and dark green.
    The evidence isolates light as the changed condition.
  • MICROSCOPIC — final meaning
    Too small to see without a tool such as a microscope.
  • Why did Plant B struggle even with water?
    PHOTO means light. Photosynthesis needs light, so water alone was not enough for the plant to make food.
    The strongest responses name light as the missing condition and tie it to the word part.
  • ACE Extend: dim light prediction
    It would grow better than Plant B but not as well as Plant A, because it gets some light but not full light.
    Accept any prediction that is justified by the pattern in the observations.

What to look for

Watch for students who revise their first prediction after reading the passage. Revision is the desired behavior, not a mistake.

Response evidence

1 — One clueIdentifies one relevant word part.
2 — Several cluesIdentifies multiple parts but does not connect them.
3 — Connected meaningCombines word structure and science context.
4 — TransferUses known word parts successfully in a new situation.
