Decode It: Hydrosphere

Grades 3–5 · Earth and Space · BOY · Articulate · Decode It · 10 min

Teacher key

  • Step 2 prediction
    Any prediction involving water and a region, layer, or ball shape.
    The prediction is graded on use of the parts, not on being correct yet.
  • Step 4 final meaning
    All of Earth's water — ocean, lakes, rivers, groundwater, ice, and water vapor in the air.
    The passage is the evidence. Students should name more than the ocean.
  • Checkboxes
    Check: the ocean; water vapor in the air; a glacier; water moving through soil. Do not check: rock deep underground.
    Rock is the geosphere. The distractor tests whether students read the passage or only the word parts.
  • ACE Extend: hydroelectric
    Electricity made using moving water. HYDRO is the shared clue.

What to look for

The target response connects the meaning to a specific sentence in the passage. A student who writes only 'water ball' has used the clue without checking the science.

Response evidence

1 — One clueIdentifies one relevant word part.
2 — Several cluesIdentifies multiple parts but does not connect them.
3 — Connected meaningCombines word structure and science context.
4 — TransferUses known word parts successfully in a new situation.
