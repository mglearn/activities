Sort It: Autotroph or Heterotroph

Grades 6–8 · Organisms and Environments · MOY · Connect · Sort It · 15 min

Teacher key

  • Autotrophs
    grass, oak tree, algae, cactus — all make their own food by photosynthesis.
    Accept any green plant or alga.
  • Heterotrophs
    rabbit, mushroom, hawk, human — all eat other living things.
    Mushroom is a common miss; fungi are heterotrophs even though they do not move.
  • What does TROPH tell you?
    Both words are about how a living thing feeds.
  • Venus flytrap
    It is an autotroph that also traps insects for extra nutrients, so it does not fit neatly in one column. The word parts alone cannot settle it — you need to know it still photosynthesizes.
    This is the reasoning item. Reward students who explain the tension, not those who just pick a column.

What to look for

Look for AUTO and HETERO used to justify placements. A student who sorts by 'plant vs animal' without the feeding idea is at level 2.

Response evidence

1 — One clueIdentifies one relevant word part.
2 — Several cluesIdentifies multiple parts but does not connect them.
3 — Connected meaningCombines word structure and science context.
4 — TransferUses known word parts successfully in a new situation.
