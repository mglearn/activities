Sort It: Genotype or Phenotype

Grades 9–12 · Biology · MOY · Connect · Sort It · 15 min

Teacher key

  • Genotype column
    the alleles Bb; the DNA sequence for a trait; TT for seed shape — all describe the genes carried.
    These are the genetic-makeup descriptions.
  • Phenotype column
    brown eyes; a six-foot-tall plant; a wrinkled pea pod — all describe visible traits.
    These are the observable descriptions.
  • What TYPE tells you
    Both words describe a kind or form — one the genetic kind, one the visible kind.
  • Same phenotype, different genotype
    BB and Bb both show purple because the dominant allele is expressed either way, so the visible trait matches while the underlying alleles differ.
    Reward the dominant-allele reasoning tied to the two word parts.
  • Hidden then reappearing trait
    Genotype — the recessive gene was carried all along even though the phenotype did not show it.
    Transfer item.

What to look for

The key behaviour is holding GENO (carried genes) apart from PHENO (shown traits). A student who sorts by 'letters vs words' without the meaning is at level 2.

Response evidence

1 — One clueIdentifies one relevant word part.
2 — Several cluesIdentifies multiple parts but does not connect them.
3 — Connected meaningCombines word structure and science context.
4 — TransferUses known word parts successfully in a new situation.
