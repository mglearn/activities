Sort It: One or Many

Grades 6–8 · Matter and Energy · MOY · Connect · Sort It · 15 min

Teacher key

  • MONO column
    monomer, monorail, monoxide, a single building-block unit — all point to one.
  • POLY column
    polymer, polygon, polyester, a long chain of repeating units — all point to many.
  • MONOMER and POLYMER share MER
    MER means unit. MONO makes it one unit; POLY makes it many units joined together.
    Look for the shared root explained, not just the two words sorted.
  • Carbon monoxide
    MONO = one, so one oxygen atom is bonded to the carbon.

What to look for

The number sense — MONO one, POLY many — should drive both the sort and the monomer/polymer explanation.

Response evidence

1 — One clueIdentifies one relevant word part.
2 — Several cluesIdentifies multiple parts but does not connect them.
3 — Connected meaningCombines word structure and science context.
4 — TransferUses known word parts successfully in a new situation.
