Word Equation: Endothermic and Exothermic

Grades 6–8 · Matter and Energy · MOY · Connect · Word Equation · 15 min

Teacher key

  • ENDOTHERMIC clue and direction
    ENDO = inside, THERM = heat, -IC = relating to; heat moves in, so the surroundings feel colder.
    Watch for the reversal: 'inside' means heat goes into the change, out of the surroundings.
  • EXOTHERMIC clue and direction
    EXO = outside, THERM = heat, -IC = relating to; heat moves out to the surroundings, which feel warmer.
  • Instant cold pack
    Endothermic. It takes heat in from your hand and the air, so it feels cold.
    Require both the word and the direction of heat.
  • Hand warmer
    Exothermic. It gives off heat to your hand.
  • Ice melting
    Endothermic. Melting takes in heat from the room.
    A good transfer check of the direction idea.

What to look for

The distinguishing behaviour is getting the direction of heat right, not just matching the label. A student who says 'endothermic means hot' has the direction backwards.

Response evidence

2 — Several cluesIdentifies multiple parts but does not connect them.
