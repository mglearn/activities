Break It: Biology

Grades 3–5 · Organisms and Environments · BOY · Articulate · Break It · 10 min

Teacher key

  • BIOLOGY
    BIO = life; LOGY = the study of; the study of living things.
    Both parts are transparent here, which is why this word is a good first break.
  • GEOLOGY
    GEO = Earth; LOGY = the study of; the study of Earth and its rocks.
  • ECOLOGY
    ECO = home or surroundings; LOGY = the study of; the study of how living things interact with their surroundings.
    Accept 'the study of the environment' if the student explains that living things are part of it.
  • What does ending in LOGY tell you about all three?
    All three are fields of study. The first part names what is being studied.
    This is the generalization the activity is built to produce.
  • Drought and prairie animals — which word?
    Ecology. The question is about how living things respond to their surroundings, not about rocks or about living things in general.
    Look for a reason, not just the right word. Biology is not wrong as a broad label, but ecology is the better fit and the student should say why.

What to look for

Students should use the first word part to justify the choice. A student who picks ecology without referring to 'surroundings' is at level 1 or 2.

Response evidence

1 — One clueIdentifies one relevant word part.
2 — Several cluesIdentifies multiple parts but does not connect them.
3 — Connected meaningCombines word structure and science context.
4 — TransferUses known word parts successfully in a new situation.
