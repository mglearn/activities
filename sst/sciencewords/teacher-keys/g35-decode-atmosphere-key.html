Decode It: Atmosphere

Grades 3–5 · Earth and Space · MOY · Connect · Decode It · 15 min

Teacher key

  • ATMOSPHERE
    The layer of gases surrounding Earth — the air, including water vapor.
  • HYDROSPHERE
    All of Earth's water.
  • GEOSPHERE
    Earth's rock and soil, at the surface and below.
  • BIOSPHERE
    All the places where living things are found.
  • Rain falling on soil — which two systems?
    The hydrosphere (the rain) and the geosphere (the soil and rock). If the student mentions plant roots, the biosphere is involved too.
    Accept three systems when the student names roots, and treat that as stronger reasoning.
  • 'The atmosphere and hydrosphere never touch' — why is that wrong?
    Water vapor from the ocean rises into the atmosphere, condenses into clouds, and falls back as rain. The two systems exchange water constantly.
    The passage supplies the whole causal chain; students should cite it.
  • ACE Extend: lithosphere
    Most like the geosphere. LITH means rock, and GEO also points to Earth's rock.

What to look for

Level 3 responses use the shared part SPHERE to explain that all four name regions of Earth, and use the first part to explain what makes each region different.

Response evidence

1 — One clueIdentifies one relevant word part.
2 — Several cluesIdentifies multiple parts but does not connect them.
3 — Connected meaningCombines word structure and science context.
4 — TransferUses known word parts successfully in a new situation.
