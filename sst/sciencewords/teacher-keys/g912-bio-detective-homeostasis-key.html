Word Detective: Homeostasis

Grades 9–12 · Biology · MOY · Connect · Word Detective · 20 min

Teacher key

  • HOMEOSTASIS
    HOMEO = steady, STASIS = staying the same; keeping the internal state balanced. Evidence: the body is pulled back toward a steady point whatever the outside conditions.
    Require the evidence column to cite the passage, not just a definition.
  • GLUCOSE
    GLUC = sweet, -OSE = sugar; the blood sugar that rises after the snack and is then brought back down.
  • Why 'balance' beats 'no change'
    HOMEO means steady or similar, not identical; the values move (temperature, glucose) but are pulled back toward a set point, so 'balance around a point' fits better than 'never changes'.
    Reward the nuance that homeostasis allows fluctuation around a steady point.
  • Two things held steady
    Body temperature (near 37 °C, held by sweating) and blood glucose (raised by the snack, lowered by the signal). Both are named in the passage.
    Require both, each with its evidence.
  • Moving into the cold
    The body shivers or narrows blood vessels to hold temperature steady — a homeostatic response, since HOMEO + STASIS means keeping the internal state steady.
    Transfer item using the parts.

What to look for

A level 3 answer uses the parts to argue for 'steady balance' and cites the passage for each variable. The cold-room item checks transfer.
