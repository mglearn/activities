Break It, Build It · Science Word Parts

Partner Challenge: Build and Trade

Grades 6–8 · Organisms and Environments · EOY · Extend · Partner Challenge · 15 min

Teacher key

  • Sample built words
    AUTO+TROPH = autotroph (self-feeder); HYDRO+LYSIS = hydrolysis (breaking with water); LITH+SPHERE = lithosphere (rock layer); ENDO+THERM = endotherm (inside heat); CYTO+PLASM = cytoplasm (cell material).
    Accept any real combination the partners can justify; this is generative, so answers vary.
  • Hardest word to predict
    Answers vary. Look for a reason tied to an unfamiliar part rather than 'it was just hard'.
    Reward metacognition about which parts were clear and which were not.

What to look for

This is a generative task, so there is no single answer key. Judge whether partners used the parts to predict meanings they had not been directly taught — that is the level 4 behaviour.

Response evidence

1 — One clueIdentifies one relevant word part.
2 — Several cluesIdentifies multiple parts but does not connect them.
3 — Connected meaningCombines word structure and science context.
4 — TransferUses known word parts successfully in a new situation.
