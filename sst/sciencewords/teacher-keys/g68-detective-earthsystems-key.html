Word Detective: Earth's Systems

Grades 6–8 · Earth and Space · MOY · Connect · Word Detective · 20 min

Teacher key

  • ATMOSPHERE
    ATMO = air/vapor, SPHERE = layer; the layer of air around the Earth. Includes the clouds and the falling rain at the start.
  • LITHOSPHERE
    LITH = rock, SPHERE = layer; the rocky layer — the bare rock and soil the rain lands on.
  • HYDROSPHERE
    HYDRO = water, SPHERE = layer; all the water — the stream the runoff joins.
  • BIOSPHERE
    BIO = life, SPHERE = layer; all living things — the plant roots and animals that take up water.
  • Raindrop path
    Atmosphere (falls as rain) → lithosphere (lands on rock/soil) → hydrosphere (runs into the stream), with the biosphere drawing water along the way.
    Order matters; reward tracing the passage rather than listing from memory.
  • Wildfire
    It touches the atmosphere (smoke into the air) and the biosphere (burns plants). Accept lithosphere too if the student mentions scorched soil.
    Transfer item using the first parts.

What to look for

A level 3 answer uses each first part to name the system. The raindrop trace shows whether the student read the passage or guessed.

Response evidence

1 — One clueIdentifies one relevant word part.
2 — Several cluesIdentifies multiple parts but does not connect them.
3 — Connected meaningCombines word structure and science context.
4 — TransferUses known word parts successfully in a new situation.
