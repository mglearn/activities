Break It, Build It · Science Word Parts

Build It: Word-Part Cards

Grades 3–5 · Organisms and Environments · BOY · Articulate · Build It · 15 min

Teacher key

  • Real words students can build
    biology, geology, hydrosphere, biosphere, geosphere, thermometer, microscope, telescope. Also acceptable with explanation: hydrology (the study of water), microbiology if students combine three cards.
  • Combinations that sound real but are not standard science terms
    therm + sphere, micro + sphere, tele + logy, hydro + scope, bio + meter (biometer is not a standard classroom science term).
    This is the productive part of the activity. Ask the student what the word would mean if it existed, then have them check it.
  • Which card did you use most?
    SPHERE and LOGY are the most reusable. Accept any card with a reason.
    Look for the idea that a suffix can attach to many roots.

What to look for

Do not mark invented words wrong outright. Ask: what would it mean, and how would you check? A student who builds hydroscope and reasons 'a tool for looking at water' is doing exactly the intended thinking.

Response evidence

1 — One clueIdentifies one relevant word part.
2 — Several cluesIdentifies multiple parts but does not connect them.
3 — Connected meaningCombines word structure and science context.
4 — TransferUses known word parts successfully in a new situation.
