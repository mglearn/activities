Word Equation: Thermometer

Grades 3–5 · Matter and Energy · BOY · Articulate · Word Equation · 10 min

Teacher key

  • THERMOMETER science meaning
    A tool used to measure temperature.
    The clue says 'heat measurer.' The science says temperature. Keep those separate on the board.
  • SPEEDOMETER clue and meaning
    Clue: a speed measurer. Science meaning: a tool that measures how fast something is moving — distance over time.
  • Why is 'a heat measurer' not a perfect definition?
    A thermometer reports temperature, which tells how hot or cold something is. It does not measure the total amount of heat energy in an object.
    This is the central teaching point of the sheet. Grade 3–5 students do not need the full heat-versus-temperature distinction, but they should see that the clue was approximate.
  • GEOTHERMAL prediction
    Heat that comes from inside Earth.
    Sets up the EOY stimulus activity.

What to look for

Look for students who can state the clue and the science meaning as two different sentences. That is the habit the whole resource is building.

Response evidence

2 — Several cluesIdentifies multiple parts but does not connect them.
3 — Connected meaningCombines word structure and science context.
