Exit Ticket: Word-Part Check (BOY)

Grades 3–5 · Organisms and Environments · BOY · Articulate · Exit Ticket · 5 min

Teacher key

  • MICRO means
    Small.
  • MICROORGANISM probably means
    A very small living thing — too small to see without a microscope.
    Accept 'a tiny living thing.'
  • What clue did MICRO give you?
    It told me the organism is small, so I would need a tool to see it.

What to look for

This is a beginning-of-year recognition check. One clear clue is a successful response at this point in the year.

Response evidence

1 — One clueIdentifies one relevant word part.
2 — Several cluesIdentifies multiple parts but does not connect them.
3 — Connected meaningCombines word structure and science context.
4 — TransferUses known word parts successfully in a new situation.
