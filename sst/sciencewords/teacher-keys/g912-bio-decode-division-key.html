Decode It: Mitosis and Meiosis

Grades 9–12 · Biology · MOY · Connect · Decode It · 20 min

Teacher key

  • MITOSIS
    MITO = thread, -OSIS = process; the division that makes two identical cells. The threads are the chromosomes.
  • MEIOSIS
    MEIO = reduction, -OSIS = process; the division that halves the chromosome number to make gametes.
  • Which reduces the number
    Meiosis. MEIO means reduction, and the table shows 46 dropping to 23.
    Require the table value, not just the word.
  • Why MEIO is more informative
    MITO ('thread') only describes how the chromosomes look, while MEIO ('reduction') names the actual change in number, which is what distinguishes the two.
    This is the reasoning item; reward the contrast between description and function.
  • Making gametes
    Meiosis, because MEIO signals the reduction to half needed for egg and sperm.
    Transfer using the word part plus the table.

What to look for

The distinguishing behaviour is tying MEIO to the halving in the table. A student who swaps the two based on sound alone has not used the parts.

Response evidence

1 — One clueIdentifies one relevant word part.
2 — Several cluesIdentifies multiple parts but does not connect them.
3 — Connected meaningCombines word structure and science context.
4 — TransferUses known word parts successfully in a new situation.
