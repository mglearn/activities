Sort It: Intra or Inter

Grades 6–8 · Organisms and Environments · MOY · Connect · Sort It · 10 min

Teacher key

  • INTRA column
    fluid inside a single cell; reactions happening inside one cell; digestion within a single cell.
  • INTER column
    The between-cells descriptions: signals moving cell to cell, the space that separates two cells, and water crossing from one leaf cell into another.
  • Difference in own words
    INTRA means within one thing; INTER means between two or more. One letter flips the meaning.
    Look for 'within' vs 'between', not vague 'inside/around'.
  • Interstate highways
    Yes — they run between states, which fits INTER.

What to look for

The key behaviour is holding INTRA (within) and INTER (between) apart. Reward everyday examples that show the student can transfer the prefixes.

Response evidence

1 — One clueIdentifies one relevant word part.
2 — Several cluesIdentifies multiple parts but does not connect them.
3 — Connected meaningCombines word structure and science context.
4 — TransferUses known word parts successfully in a new situation.
