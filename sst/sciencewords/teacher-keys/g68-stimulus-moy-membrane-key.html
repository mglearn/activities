Decode the Stimulus: The Selective Membrane (MOY)

Grades 6–8 · Organisms and Environments · MOY · Connect · Decode the Stimulus · 20 min

Teacher key

  • Break it
    SEMI = partly; PERMEABLE = able to be passed through.
  • Predict
    A membrane that only partly lets things through.
    Accept predictions near 'lets some things through'.
  • Passed and blocked
    Passed: water and glucose (small). Blocked: starch and the dye clump (large). Final meaning: a membrane that lets small particles through but stops large ones.
    Require the size pattern from the table, not just 'some things'.
  • Why semipermeable not permeable
    The membrane passes small particles and blocks large ones; a fully permeable membrane would let everything through.
    Correct answer is the first option.
  • Why useful to a cell
    It can take in water and small nutrients while keeping large molecules inside, so it controls what enters and leaves.
    Transfer of the idea to cell function.

What to look for

The decode behaviour is linking SEMI to the table's size pattern. A student who ignores size and says 'lets some through' is at level 2.
