About the Learning Activities Hub

Free, self-contained classroom activities — built to run entirely in a student's browser, in seven languages, with no logins and no data collected.

What this is

The Learning Activities Hub is a growing home for free classroom activities — reasoning breakouts, literature rooms, science investigations, lab-safety modules, and more. Everything runs entirely in a student's browser, in seven languages, with no logins, no accounts, and no data collected.

Content curator & developer

Miguel Guhlin curates the texts and topics, writes and organizes the learning content, and develops the activities themselves — from reading selections, vocabulary, and standards alignment to the breakouts, games, analysis tasks, and the seven-language translations. Each activity is built to be privacy-first and classroom-ready: it collects nothing, sends nothing, and works on any modern device.

Miguel Guhlin is a longtime educator and educational-technology writer focused on teaching, learning, and equitable access to technology.

How it's made & shared

Original content is licensed CC BY 4.0; the code is MIT. Public-domain texts are used and linked directly; copyrighted works are studied through paraphrase only and are never reproduced. The seven-language translations are machine-assisted and flagged for local educator review.
