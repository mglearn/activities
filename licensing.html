Provenance

The Learning Activities Hub grew out of TCEA's professional-learning work. Its suites include Critical Thinking Online Breakouts (CLEAR, Fourth of July, July 5 & Black Freedom Holidays, Bible as Literature, and Science K–8), PlotPoint literature rooms, Problem-Solving Teaching (PST), Say It Make It, Lab Safety Certification, Relic Rooms, Digital Math Tools, Digital Citizenship, GenAI Literacy, and Suffrage Sojourn.

Suffrage Sojourn is a no-combat history journey through five turning points in the movement for voting rights, published separately at mglearn.github.io/sojourn and offered in four languages (English, Español, Tiếng Việt, and 中文). Its historical material comes from public institutional sources — the Library of Congress exhibition on the Declaration of Sentiments, the National Archives milestone documents for the 19th Amendment and the Voting Rights Act, and the National Park Service suffrage timeline — each linked directly from the source shelf in teacher mode. Quoted primary-source passages are short excerpts from public-domain documents; the scene artwork is original illustration in the style of a period archival print, not reproduced historical artwork.

Shared instructional scaffolding — UDL guidance, ELPS supports, and lesson-plan structure — is adapted from TCEA breakout and professional-development materials. Several suites are distilled from TCEA workshop curricula.

Content, code, artwork, and the translations are produced with the help of generative-AI tools and refined under Miguel Guhlin's direction and editorial review; a human stays in the loop on every decision. Translations are machine-assisted and flagged for local educator review before classroom use.

Privacy

Every activity runs entirely in the student's browser. There are no logins and no accounts, nothing is uploaded, and no student data is collected or transmitted. Progress, where an activity saves any, stays on the device.
