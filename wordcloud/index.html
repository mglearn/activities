Privacy, accessibility & how it works

The tool processes your text and analysis in browser memory. It uses no analytics, cookies, external fonts, AI services, or network requests for your work. Nothing is saved automatically. Closing or refreshing the page clears your work. Use Save project to download a JSON copy; opening that file restores it. Downloaded files remain on your device until you delete them. The hosted page itself is served by the hosting provider. External source links open only when you choose them.

Counts ignore capitalization and use Unicode letters, including accented characters. English possessive endings are removed. Other word forms are counted separately. Filtered totals exclude common words and your exclusions; minimum frequency filters the word list after counting. The cloud shows up to the chosen maximum; some words may not fit. The table and CSV include every word meeting the filters. Every cloud word is keyboard accessible; the frequency table provides the same information in text. PNG exports use a white background. No accessibility or legal certification is claimed.
