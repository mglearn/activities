# PlotPoint — Image prompts for the 8 Texas-2026 speech/document rooms (Phase 2b)

64 images: **8 heroes** (square, 1024×1024) + **56 relic artifacts** (portrait 4:5,
1024×1280). Export **WebP**. Paths are exact; place each file at
`plotpoint/<room>/images/<file>`.

## Shared style and safety rules

Warm painterly editorial illustration, historically grounded period detail, muted palette,
soft directional light, shallow depth of field, and the room accent subtly tinting the
light. Symbolic objects, landscapes, silhouettes, backs of heads, or non-identifiable
figures only—no photoreal likenesses of historical people, readable quotations, modern
logos, watermarks, gore, battle action, or partisan campaign imagery. These are clearly
instructional reconstructions, not archival photographs or artwork from an edition.
