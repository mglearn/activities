## ⚠️ Style & sensitivity rules (read first)
These passages are studied **as literature** in a **neutral, academic, non-devotional**
frame. Generate illustrations that match that:
- **No real faces / no photoreal people.** Use symbolic objects, landscapes, silhouettes,
  hands, or stylized figures seen from behind / in shadow. Never depict a deity or a
  divine figure's face; suggest presence through light, not a body.
- **No sectarian iconography** (no halos, crosses, specific religious insignia). Keep it
  literary and universal — think "museum teaching illustration / storyboard concept art,"
  clearly an **instructional reconstruction**, not artwork from any edition.
- Warm, painterly, muted palette; soft directional light; shallow depth of field.
- Each room's accent color can tint the light (see per-room note).
