The CLT Reading Room

Public-domain classics from the classical reading canon — the kind of works featured on the Classic Learning Test (CLT) — as self-contained PlotPoint learning rooms, organized by grade level. Every room includes the free full text and an audiobook, works in seven languages, and collects no student data.

Note: These rooms are aligned to the classical / CLT reading canon and are not affiliated with or endorsed by the Classic Learning Test. Every title here is in the public domain; suggested grade ranges are starting points — review for your students.
