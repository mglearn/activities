# PlotPoint — Poster prompts for the actual collection

Literary-analysis **poster prompts for texts that ARE PlotPoint rooms** — one per live room, generated from each room's own themes, symbols, ACE tasks, and accent color. Same PlotPoint house style and ACE badge as `posters.md`.

> **How to use:** paste the **Shared Style Block** (below) first, then a room's **Prompt** block. Save each finished poster as **`<room-id>.webp`** (the id is shown per entry) into `posters/img/`. Naming a poster after its room id lets the hub table auto-link it in the **Poster** column for that room.

> Posters analyze the works and reproduce **no** copyrighted text or cover art; callouts paraphrase. Some titles are frequently challenged — confirm against your campus list before printing.

---

## Shared Style Block (paste at the top of every prompt)

> Design a print-ready **vertical 18×24 in classroom poster** in the **PlotPoint house style**. **Core palette (match exactly):** deep navy `#1C2247` and `#12163A` backgrounds; plum `#7A4FCE` and `#5A34A6` accents; bright gold `#F5C542` highlights; soft lavender-white `#F5F4FB` text panels; add the room's **signature accent color** (given per poster) as the focal pop over the navy/plum base. A small **gold dot with a soft glow** as the PlotPoint mark near the title. **Type:** bold rounded display face for the title (Fredoka-like); clean humanist sans for body (Nunito-like); all text crisp, legible, correctly spelled. **Layout, top→bottom:** (1) bold **title banner** with the work's title + author; (2) large **central symbolic illustration** (original instructional art — no copyrighted covers/stills); (3) **2–3 gold-ribboned analysis-callout tags**; (4) a compact **ACE badge**: three plum chips — **A · Articulate**, **C · Connect**, **E · Extend** — each with one short line. High contrast, generous margins, a little flair.

**ACE, in one line:** *Articulate* an idea in your own words → *Connect* it to evidence and other ideas → *Extend* it to a new text or situation.
