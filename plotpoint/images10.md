# PlotPoint — Image prompts for the 7 Texas-2026 HS poem rooms (Phase 1b, batch 2)

56 images: **7 heroes** (square, 1024×1024) + **49 relic artifacts** (portrait 4:5, 1024×1280).
Export **WebP**. Paths exact — drop each at `plotpoint/<room>/images/<file>`.

## Style rules
Warm, painterly, muted palette; soft directional light; shallow depth of field. **No real
faces / no photoreal identifiable people** — symbolic objects, silhouettes, hands,
landscapes. Clearly an **instructional reconstruction / concept illustration**. Each room's
accent tints the light.

---

### thanatopsis — Bryant (accent green-grey #4a5a4a)
- `hero.webp` — a vast serene landscape at dusk — hills, a river, distant woods — feeling like a peaceful resting place; calm, consoling.
- `voice.webp` — an open sky and quiet woods, a figure (silhouette) listening to Nature's teaching.
- `shudder.webp` — a dark, cold image of a shroud/pall and "breathless darkness" the poem answers.
- `earth.webp` — soil, roots, and stone receiving a form back into the elements.
- `sepulchre.webp` — the whole earth pictured as one great tomb: hills and valleys as its ornaments.
- `company.webp` — countless faint silhouettes of past ages — patriarchs, kings, the wise — at rest together.
- `caravan.webp` — a long "innumerable caravan" of shadowy figures moving toward pale realms.
- `dreams.webp` — a figure lying down peacefully as if to "pleasant dreams," draped calmly.

### lightbrigade — Tennyson (accent blood-red #6b3a2f)

- `gallop.webp` — horses at full gallop, dust and rhythm — "half a league onward."
- `blunder.webp` — a dropped/mistaken order paper on the ground before the charge.
