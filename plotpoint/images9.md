# PlotPoint — Image prompts for the 6 Texas-2026 HS poem rooms (Phase 1b, batch 1)

48 images: **6 heroes** (square, 1024×1024) + **42 relic artifacts** (portrait 4:5, 1024×1280).
Export **WebP**. Paths exact — drop each at `plotpoint/<room>/images/<file>`.

## Style rules
Warm, painterly, muted palette; soft directional light; shallow depth of field. **No real
faces / no photoreal identifiable people** — symbolic objects, silhouettes, hands,
landscapes. Clearly an **instructional reconstruction / concept illustration**, not artwork
from any edition. Each room's accent tints the light.

---

### ozymandias — "Ozymandias" · Shelley (accent stone #6b6250)
- `hero.webp` — a colossal ruined stone statue half-buried in a vast empty desert at dusk; two broken legs and a fallen shattered head on the sand.
- `traveler.webp` — a lone traveler silhouette crossing an endless desert, a distant ruin ahead (the frame tale).
- `legs.webp` — "two vast and trunkless legs of stone" standing alone in sand.
- `face.webp` — a shattered stone face half-sunk in sand, brow frowning, a "sneer of cold command."
- `sculptor.webp` — a sculptor's chisel and hand marks on weathered stone (art that outlasts the tyrant).
- `inscription.webp` — a cracked pedestal carved with a proud inscription (illegible/decorative — no real text).
- `irony.webp` — the tiny ruin dwarfed by the immense empty desert around it (boast vs. reality).
- `sands.webp` — "the lone and level sands stretch far away" — boundless bare dunes to the horizon.

### ifpoem — "If—" · Kipling (accent steel-blue #3f5a6b)
- `hero.webp` — a steady figure (silhouette) standing calm on a rocky height as a storm breaks around them; composure.
- `refrain.webp` — an open hand offering advice, a path of stepping-stones behind it ("If you can…").
- `head.webp` — one calm silhouette amid a blurred, panicking crowd (keeping your head).
- `impostors.webp` — two identical theatrical masks labeled by expression only — triumph and disaster treated the same.
- `dreams.webp` — a person holding a kite/lantern loosely, not letting it pull them away (dreams without being ruled by them).

- `will.webp` — a lone hand gripping a rope over a drop, holding on when nothing else remains.
- `reward.webp` — a wide horizon opening at dawn from a summit; the earned, withheld reward.

### wemask — "We Wear the Mask" · Dunbar (accent plum #4a3f4a)
- `hero.webp` — a smooth smiling theatrical mask held before shadow, a hint of sorrow behind it; restrained, dignified.
- `mask.webp` — a single grinning mask, polished on the front, worn on the inside (the central symbol).
- `grin.webp` — the bright, false smiling surface the world is shown.
- `hearts.webp` — behind the mask, a dim shape of a wounded heart (hidden pain). Symbolic, not graphic.
- `we.webp` — many identical masks in a row, suggesting a shared, communal experience ("we").
- `world.webp` — a crowd of faceless onlookers seeing only the smile, turned away from the pain.
- `cry.webp` — a lifted face-shape in shadow, a silent cry upward from behind the mask.
