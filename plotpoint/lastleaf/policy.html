Privacy & Compliance

This PlotPoint room is safe for classroom use by design: it collects nothing and sends nothing anywhere.

What we collect

Nothing. No accounts, no logins, no analytics, no tracking cookies, and no data sent to any server. Everything runs inside the student's browser. The only things stored are the student's own progress, their typed responses, the Gen AI on/off preference, and the language choice — kept in that browser's localStorage (keys plotpoint.lastleaf.v1 and tcea.breakouts.lang), which never leave the device. The Reset button erases saved progress at any time.

Outside requests

The page loads only Google Fonts for typography; all activity code, content, and the seven-language translations are served from the same site, with <meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer"> set. No student writing is ever transmitted. The optional Gen AI panel provides copyable prompts only — it does not connect to any AI service, and it warns students never to paste names or private information into district-approved tools they open separately.

A note on student work

Every response a student types stays in their own browser and appears only on the page they choose to print. Nothing is submitted or seen by anyone else. Follow your district's policies for any printed artifacts students create.

How this maps to the rules that protect students

Texas Student Data Privacy Agreement (TX-NDPA / SDPC)
The activity collects, stores, and transmits no personally identifiable student information and creates no student records off the device, so it falls outside the scope that requires a signed Data Privacy Agreement.
Texas SCOPE Act — Texas Education Code §32.151–32.156
No operator data collection, targeted advertising, profiling, or monitoring of minors.
FERPA (20 U.S.C. §1232g)
No education records are created, stored, or disclosed by these pages.
COPPA
No personal information is collected from children — there is nothing to collect.
GDPR / UK GDPR
No personal data is processed, so there is nothing to access, export, rectify, or erase.
CIPA
Static, self-contained educational content with no user-generated communication or open web browsing.

Accessibility (VPAT-lite)

  • Semantic headings, keyboard-operable controls, visible focus outlines, and focus moved to the activity region on section change.
  • Correct and incorrect answers are shown with text and a color bar, never color alone.
  • The arcade game offers per-card buttons, so it never requires drag-and-drop; there are no timers and no lives system.
  • Live regions announce progress; full right-to-left support for Arabic and Urdu; seven languages via a device-local switcher.
  • Reduced-motion support, responsive to small phones, and a print stylesheet for the student packet.

Text, copyright & standards

The Last Leaf (1907) by O. Henry is in the public domain. This room does not reproduce the full story and invents no quotations. The free text and audiobook links (Project Gutenberg, LibriVox) point to the public-domain The Trimmed Lamp collection that contains the story — verify before assigning. Relic-room artifacts are clearly labeled instructional reconstructions, not copyrighted illustrations. Content is aligned to, not reproduced from, the standards listed in the Teacher Guide; TEKS and ELPS alignments are marked Needs review for local confirmation.
