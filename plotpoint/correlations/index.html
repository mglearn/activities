Texas TEKS · English Language Arts & Reading

Across grades 6–12, PlotPoint rooms address the recurring ELAR strands (19 TAC Chapter 110). Specific student expectations vary by grade and course — confirm against your adoption.

  • Developing & sustaining foundational language skills — vocabulary study, listening, and academic discussion.
  • Comprehension — self-monitoring, making inferences, and using evidence (Relic Room, Crack the Case).
  • Response skills — interacting with sources and responding through writing and discussion (Analyze, Extend).
  • Author's purpose & craft — analyzing structure, point of view, and rhetorical choices (Analyze, Arcade).
  • Composition — planning and writing evidence-based responses (Analyze, Extend, Print).
  • Inquiry & research — generating questions and gathering evidence, including responsible tool use (Gen AI Partner).
  • Multiple genres — fiction, drama, poetry, memoir, argument, and informational texts across the collection.

Texas ELPS · English Language Proficiency Standards

PlotPoint is built to widen access for emergent bilingual students, supporting all four ELPS language domains.

Listening

Read-aloud options, audiobooks (public-domain rooms), and clear spoken-language routines.

Speaking

Discussion prompts, arcade justifications, and partner interviews in the Gen AI alternative.

Reading

Seven-language chrome, visuals, vocabulary supports, and evidence-based comprehension tasks.

Writing

Scaffolded short and extended responses, organizers, and a printable packet.

Seven-language interface (English, Spanish, Vietnamese, Arabic, Hindi, Urdu, Chinese) with full right-to-left support for Arabic and Urdu widens access at every proficiency level.

Every room is designed around the three UDL principles.
