The five-challenge pattern

Every pack mixes these five kinds of thinking. Any one challenge also works on its own in a few minutes.

1 · Guess ItIdentify something from four progressively easier clues.
2 · Sort ItClassify cards into categories — including a "depends" option when nuance matters.
3 · Spot ItNotice, wonder, and reason from an image, map, or pattern.
4 · Explain ItMake a claim from a small evidence set and say what it can and cannot support.
5 · Extend ItCreate, design, or apply the idea in a new context.
