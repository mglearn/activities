TCEA Curiosity Challenge

Privacy

Every Curiosity Challenge activity is built to be safe to use with students on day one — no accounts, no sign-in, nothing to set up.

These activities run in your browser. They do not ask for student names, accounts, or personal information. Responses are not sent to a server.

What these activities do

  • Run entirely on the device, in the web browser.
  • Let students type answers, notes, and reflections that stay on screen only.
  • Work with no login and no account of any kind.

What they do not do

  • Collect student names or email addresses.
  • Require or create an account.
  • Store student responses on a server or in the cloud.
  • Send typed answers to analytics or any third party.
  • Track individual students across sessions.

What happens to what students type

Anything a student types into a challenge — a guess, an evidence note, a claim, a design — lives only in the page while it is open. Using the Reset button, reloading, or closing the tab clears it. Nothing is saved for later unless the student prints or writes it down on purpose.

Fonts & hosting

The pages load a web font from Google Fonts and, when published on the Learning Activities Hub, a shared licensing footer from the same site. No student input is ever sent to either. If you prefer zero third-party requests, use the printable student pack instead.

Printing

The printable student and teacher pages are plain HTML you can print or save as PDF locally. They contain no tracking and require no connection once loaded.
